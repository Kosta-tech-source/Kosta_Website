God of War

God of War is a game where you follow Kratos, a Greek god who destroyed Olympus and now simply wants to live a peaceful life. Together with his son, he must bring the ashes of his wife to the highest mountain in the world.

God of War is one of my favorite games because I love both Norse and Greek mythology. I also enjoy the rhythm of the combat — Kratos’ aggressive and brutal fighting style feels amazing.

My Favorite Moments

Meeting the World Serpent for the First Time

The Fight with Baldur

My favorite part about this fight is how intense it is — we just beat each other up while being introduced to all the combat mechanics.
